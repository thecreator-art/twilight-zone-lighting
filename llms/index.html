Frequently Asked Questions

How much does permanent outdoor lighting cost in Fresno?
Pricing starts at $950 for a Starter front-accent install. Most single-story Fresno homes range $2,800-$5,800. Two-story homes typically run $5,800-$9,000. Estate and commercial installs start at $7,500. Financing from $89/month at 0% APR for 12 months.
How long does a permanent outdoor lighting install take?
Most residential installs complete in a single day (6-8 hours). Two-story homes take 1-2 days. The W-2 in-house crew arrives at 8 AM and finishes by sunset on most single-story builds.
Are permanent outdoor lights visible during the day?
No. The aluminum track is color-matched to your trim — white, bronze, brown, or black — and tucked under the eave. Invisible from the curb during daylight.
What is the warranty on Twilight Zone permanent lighting?
Lifetime on track. 5 years on LEDs and workmanship. 60-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied within 60 days, every fixture is pulled and 100% refunded.
Will permanent outdoor lights raise my electric bill?
Negligibly. Roughly the same draw as a single ceiling fan. Most customers see $3-$8/month added.
Can I control the lights from my phone?
Yes. The companion app (iOS and Android) controls 16 million colors, 200+ patterns, scheduling, zoning, and music sync. Compatible with Alexa, Google Home, Control 4, Nice Elan.
Is permanent lighting cheaper than annual Christmas-light service?
Over 10 years, yes. A Standard install at $2,800 vs typical Fresno Christmas-light services at $800-$1,800/year ($8,000-$18,000 over 10 years).
Will the lights survive Fresno heat?
Yes. IP67 weather-rated, tested -40°F to 140°F. The same chips perform in Phoenix summers and Minnesota winters.
Do you handle HOA submittals?
Yes. We provide spec sheets and 3D renderings for HOA submittals at no charge. We have installed in most Fresno-area HOAs without amendment.
What cities does Twilight Zone Permanent Lighting serve?
Fresno (HQ), Clovis, Madera, Visalia, Hanford, Selma, Sanger, Reedley, Kingsburg, Parlier, Fowler, and Kerman. Service radius is 60 miles from our Fresno shop. Free on-site estimates anywhere in that radius.
