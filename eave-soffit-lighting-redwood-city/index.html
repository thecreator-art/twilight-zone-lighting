Do you serve Redwood Shores and Emerald Hills?
Yes. We install across all Redwood City neighborhoods including Redwood Shores, Emerald Hills, Farm Hill, Woodside Plaza, Mount Carmel, Roosevelt, and surrounding ZIPs 94059, 94061, 94062, 94063, 94064, 94065.
Will the lights survive Redwood City summers?
Yes. The track is rated IP67 and tested -40°F to 140°F — engineered for 82°F+ Redwood City summers. Redwood City earns its "Climate Best by Government Test" motto by sitting in a fog shadow, which means more clear sky and a heavier annual UV load on south-facing fascia than the coast sees. UV-stable lensing matters here, and out on the Redwood Shores lagoons the brackish air adds a corrosion factor most inland systems never face.
How long is the install in Redwood City?
Single-story homes finish in 6-8 hours. Two-story typically 1-2 days. Redwood City is served by our San Francisco & Peninsula install crew on a scheduled route — the same W-2 installers, the same RGBIC-RD hardware, and the same warranty we run in the Central Valley. The same crew stays on-site through the whole install.
Will eave lighting damage my roof?
No. Track mounts into the soffit substructure with stainless screws — same anchoring as gutters. Removal leaves only thumbtack-sized holes.
