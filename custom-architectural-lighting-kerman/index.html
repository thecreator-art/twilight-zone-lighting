Custom Architectural Lighting for Kerman homes.

Custom architectural lighting is our highest-tier residential service. We design bespoke installs for custom estates — the kind of lighting that integrates with the architecture, not just attached to it.

Architecturally, Kerman runs the gamut: Kerman West stucco ranchers, Old Kerman two-story Mediterranean builds, and the older bungalows around Kerman East with deep porch overhangs. Architectural Lighting reads differently on each — a tract single-story takes ~80 linear feet of track on the front facade, while a custom build with multiple roof planes can need 200+ feet across three to five independent zones.

We design every Kerman job around what the home actually needs, not a flat per-foot price.
