About this service

Permanent outdoor lights are the most significant residential exterior upgrade of the past decade. A continuous aluminum track — color-matched to your trim — houses individually-addressable RGBIC-RD LEDs that produce all 16 million colors and 200+ patterns. Once installed, the system is essentially invisible by day and unforgettable by night.

Where this is used

Architectural accent

Adjustable warm whites (2700K-5000K) highlight your home's best features year-round.

Holiday rotation

Christmas, Halloween, July 4th, Diwali, Valentine's, St. Patrick's — auto-scheduled by date.

Game day

Bulldogs red-and-blue, Lakers purple-and-gold, your school's colors triggered by event time.

Motion-triggered cool-white floods + scheduled deterrent lighting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do permanent outdoor lights cost?
Starter installs from $950 (single-facade, ~30 ft, one zone). Most single-story homes range $2,800-$5,800. Two-story: $5,800-$9,000. Estate and commercial: $7,500+.
Are permanent outdoor lights worth it?
Over 10 years, yes — significantly. A standard install at $2,800 vs. typical Fresno-area annual Christmas-light services at $800-$1,800/year ($8,000-$18,000 over 10 years). Permanent lights also cover 200+ holidays beyond Christmas.
Are permanent outdoor lights visible during the day?
No. The aluminum track is color-matched to your trim (white, bronze, black, brown) and tucks tightly under the eave. From the curb at noon, you'd never know they're there.
What's the warranty?
Lifetime warranty on aluminum track. 5-year on LEDs and workmanship. Plus 60-day money-back guarantee.
