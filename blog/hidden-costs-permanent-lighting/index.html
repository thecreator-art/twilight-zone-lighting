Hidden Costs of Permanent Lighting (And How to Avoid Them)

Three real hidden costs and two myths. Ask about the real ones before you sign anything.

Real #1: Electrical work

Some installs need a new dedicated 15A circuit. Costs $250-$450 if your panel has an open slot. $600-$1,200 if a sub-panel is needed. Ask the installer to confirm circuit availability before quoting.

Our quotes always include any required electrical work. Some competitors quote it separately later.

Real #2: HOA submittal fees

Some HOAs charge $25-$150 to review architectural change requests. We provide spec sheets and 3D renderings free, but the HOA's own fee is on the homeowner.

Real #3: App-platform subscription

The Jellyfish app and basic features are free forever. There is no subscription tier in our system. Beware of brands that put pattern libraries behind a monthly fee — read the fine print.

Two myths

'Roof access fees': we don't charge them. 'Color-customization upcharge': all 16M colors are included from day one — no in-app purchases.
