What to Expect on Permanent Lighting Install Day

Single-story typical day. Two-story adds 4-6 hours and sometimes a second day.

Hour 1: site prep

Crew arrives 7:30-8am. Walk the property with you. Confirm track placement, color, controller location. Drop tarps under any work area. Set up ladders.

Hours 2-5: track install

Aluminum channel screwed into soffit substructure. Stainless-steel screws every 24 inches. We work front to back, completing one section before moving to the next.

Loud-ish for the first hour (drill driver). Quiet after that.

Hours 6-7: LED + wiring

LED strip seated into the channel. Data + power cable run inside the channel, dropped through one small attic hole, terminated at the control box.

Control box mounted (usually inside garage). Wired to the dedicated breaker.

Hour 8: app setup + walkthrough

Pair the controller with your home Wi-Fi. Pair the app on your phone. Walk through scenes, schedules, smart-home integration. Test every section.

We don't leave until you've changed scenes from your phone successfully.
