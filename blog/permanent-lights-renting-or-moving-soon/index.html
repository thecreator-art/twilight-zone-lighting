Should You Get Permanent Lights If You're Moving Soon?

Inside 24 months, the math gets harder. Inside 12 months, it usually doesn't work. Here's how to decide.

The 24-month rule of thumb

At Standard tier ($2,800), you need to either (a) recover most of it in resale, or (b) avoid 2+ years of seasonal-installer fees. If you do both, it works. If neither, it usually doesn't.

When it still works

You currently pay $1,200+/year for a seasonal hang. You're going to enjoy it for 12-18 months, not pay it next year, and likely recover $800-$1,500 in faster-sale value. Net: roughly break-even — and you got a year of premium curb appeal.

When to wait

You haven't been paying for seasonal hangs and you're listing in 6 months. Just wait, sell, and install at the next house. Easier.
