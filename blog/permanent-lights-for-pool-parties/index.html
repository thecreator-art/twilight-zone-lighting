Permanent Lights for Backyard Pool Parties

Backyard installs around the pool deck or pergola turn an evening party into something people actually post about.

Where to install

Pool-deck rail, pergola underside, eave above the patio. Aim is to wash the area in color without putting LEDs at face level.

Scenes

Sunset gradient (orange-pink slow fade): cocktail hour. Music sync (any color, beat-driven flash): when the speakers come on. Steady deep purple: late-night vibe.
