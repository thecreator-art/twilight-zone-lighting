Some LEDs Look a Different Color Than the Rest

Almost always one of three causes. Here's how to diagnose without calling support first.

Cause 1: Pattern animation

A few patterns intentionally have variable color across the strip — fire effect, gradient wash. If it only happens during specific scenes, that's by design.

Switch to solid white. If all LEDs match, it was the pattern.

Cause 2: Voltage drop on long runs

On runs over 50 ft, the LEDs at the far end can look slightly dimmer/yellower than the start. The control box adjusts for this with 'voltage compensation' — toggle it on in app settings if it's off.

If the issue persists, your installer may need to add a power injection point. Free service call under our workmanship warranty.

Cause 3: Failed pixel

A single LED stuck on the wrong color is usually a hardware failure. Covered under the 5-year LED warranty. We'll replace the affected segment at no charge.
