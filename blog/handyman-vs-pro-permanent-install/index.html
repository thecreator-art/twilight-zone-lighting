Handyman vs Pro Installer for Permanent Lights

Some handymen do solid work. The warranty + liability question is where the math breaks.

What a handyman might charge

Labor only: $40-$70/hr. Total install on a Standard scope: $800-$1,400 labor + your hardware cost. Adds up close to a dealer's installed price once you account for time and bad cuts.

Where it falls apart

Manufacturer warranties typically require dealer install for full coverage. A handyman install may void the LED warranty entirely.

Liability: if a ladder slips and damages your fascia, a licensed contractor's insurance covers it. A handyman's insurance often doesn't.

When a handyman makes sense

You're using a DIY-rated kit (Govee) and want help with the ladder work. Handyman labor + DIY hardware can land at $700 total. Just don't expect manufacturer warranty support if something fails.
