Game Day Lights for Fresno State Bulldogs Fans

Bulldogs colors auto-trigger every Saturday in season. Here's how it works.

The basic setup

Pull the Bulldogs preset. Set a recurring schedule: Saturdays in football season, 4 hours before kickoff through 2 hours after. App handles the rest.

Pattern is roughly 60/40 red and blue with a slow chase animation — readable as Bulldogs from the curb without being garish.

Game-day-only escalation

Win mode: solid red with a slow pulse for the next 24 hours after a Bulldogs win. Trigger manually after final score.

Conference-game mode: brighter, faster animation for ranked matchups.

Other Central Valley teams

Lakers (purple-gold), Warriors (blue-gold), Giants (orange-black), 49ers (red-gold), Dodgers (blue-white) all have presets in the same app. Pick your team. Schedule. Done.
