How to Pitch Permanent Lights to Your HOA (And Win)

We've submitted 200+ HOA packages. Approved on first read about 90% of the time. Here's the playbook.

Submit a complete package

Spec sheet (we provide). 3D rendering of your home with the system installed (we provide). Track color sample matched to your trim. Annual cost statement (electricity). Warranty document.

HOAs reject 'incomplete submissions' more often than they reject the actual project. Don't give them an easy 'no.'

Lead with 'invisible by day'

The thing HOAs worry about is exterior visual change. The headline of your submission should be 'aluminum track color-matched to existing trim, hidden under the eave, indistinguishable from existing fascia from the curb.' Photo of a daytime install drives this home.

Mention HOAs that approved it

Granville, Woodward Lake, Copper River Ranch, Fancher Creek, Old Fig Garden Property Owners — all approved Twilight Zone installs. List the ones near you. Social proof works on boards.

Schedule preferences

Volunteer to limit holiday schedule (ex: Christmas only Nov 25-Jan 6, Halloween only Oct 15-Nov 1). Some HOAs ask. Beat them to it.
