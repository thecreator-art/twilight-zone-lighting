How is this different from Govee permanent outdoor lights?

Govee is a DIY product — you buy the kit, climb your own ladder, drill into your fascia, run your own wiring, and hope it doesn't sag in 18 months. Twilight Zone is professional installation: aluminum track color-matched to your trim, concealed wiring, lifetime track warranty, 60-day money-back guarantee, and a real local Fresno team that handles HOA submittal, financing, and permits. Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights run $400–$800 for the kit alone — by the time you account for ladders, mistakes, and the time investment, our Starter package at $950 (fully installed) is comparable in cost and dramatically better in result. We also use commercial-grade RGBIC-RD LEDs with deeper color and brighter output than Govee's consumer chips.

Do permanent outdoor lights damage your roof or fascia?

No. Our aluminum track is anchored with stainless-steel screws into the soffit substructure — the same structural anchoring used for gutters. There's no damage to siding, stucco, shingles, or trim. If the system is ever removed, the screw holes are smaller than a thumbtack and easily caulked. We carry full liability insurance and back our workmanship for 5 years.

Can permanent outdoor lights be used for Halloween, July 4th, and other holidays?

Yes — that's the entire point. Our system includes 200+ preset patterns for every major holiday: Christmas, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Diwali, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day, July 4th, Memorial Day, Labor Day, plus team colors for game day. You can also create custom patterns and schedule them automatically — every Friday turns into Bulldogs red & blue, every December turns into Christmas, sunset triggers warm-white accent.

How do permanent christmas lights work?

Permanent christmas lights are individually-addressable RGBIC-RD LEDs mounted in a continuous aluminum track that's color-matched to your home's trim and concealed under the eave. A small weatherproof control box (typically mounted in the garage or attic) connects the system to your home Wi-Fi, allowing you to control all 16 million colors and 200+ patterns from the companion app — or via Alexa, Google Home, Control 4, or Nice Elan voice commands. The system is on a schedule (sunset on, midnight off, holiday auto-rotate) and uses approximately the same power as a single ceiling fan.
